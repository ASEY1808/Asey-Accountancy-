1. About this Privacy Policy

Asey Accountancy Ltd respects the privacy and confidentiality of personal and business information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information in connection with our accounting, financial management, management reporting and related professional services.

2. Information we may collect and process

The information we process depends upon the services we provide and may include:

  • names and business contact details;
  • company and business information;
  • accounting and financial records;
  • transaction information;
  • customer and supplier information;
  • invoices, bills and payment information;
  • payroll or other financial information where relevant to the services provided;
  • correspondence and information supplied to us by clients; and
  • other information reasonably required to provide our professional services.

3. How we obtain information

Information may be provided directly by clients, their employees, representatives or other authorised persons.

Where authorised by a client, we may also access accounting and financial information through accounting software, financial systems and other business applications used by the client.

4. Accounting software and financial systems

Where authorised by a client, Asey Accountancy Ltd may access and process accounting and financial information through third-party accounting software and related financial systems.

This information may be used for accounting, management reporting, financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting and other professional services agreed with the client.

Access to client accounting systems is undertaken only where appropriate authorisation has been provided.

5. How we use information

We may use information for purposes including:

  • providing accounting and financial management services;
  • preparing management accounts and financial reports;
  • financial analysis and business performance reporting;
  • budgeting and forecasting;
  • cash flow reporting and analysis;
  • maintaining accounting and financial records;
  • communicating with clients;
  • administering our professional relationship with clients;
  • complying with legal, regulatory and professional obligations; and
  • maintaining and improving our financial reporting processes.

Asey Accountancy Ltd does not sell client accounting, financial or personal information.

6. Legal basis for processing personal information

Where UK data protection legislation applies, we process personal information only where there is an appropriate legal basis for doing so.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include processing necessary for the performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate business interests, or where consent has been provided and is the appropriate legal basis.

7. Information security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Access to client financial information and business systems is restricted to authorised persons where appropriate.

8. Third-party service providers

We may use reputable third-party service providers and technology platforms where necessary to operate our business and provide our services.

These may include accounting software providers, cloud and data storage services, email and communication systems, financial reporting technologies and other professional business systems.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate data protection and security arrangements are in place.

9. Sharing information

We do not disclose personal or confidential client information to third parties except where necessary to provide agreed services, where authorised by the client, where required by law or regulation, or where another lawful basis for disclosure applies.

10. International data transfers

Some technology providers we use may process or store information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are used where required by applicable data protection legislation.

11. Data retention

We retain personal and business information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide our services and to comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and professional requirements.

12. Your data protection rights

Under UK data protection legislation, individuals may have rights in relation to their personal information, including the right to request access to personal data and, in appropriate circumstances, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing.

The availability of these rights depends upon the circumstances and the applicable legal basis for processing.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first using the details below.

You may also have the right to raise a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices. The current version will be published on this website.
